Frequently asked questions

How do I join a lobby?

Press Join Game on a lobby card. Your browser opens Age of Empires II: DE and joins that lobby; the game needs to be installed and you need to be signed in. Games that have already started show In Progress instead.

Which ELO is shown for each player?

By default the Unranked rating is shown, the same default aoe2lobby.com uses. Use Elo Type to switch to Random Map 1v1, Random Map Team, Empire Wars 1v1 or Empire Wars Team, or to Auto, which picks the ladder that matches the lobby.

Can I hide Dark Age or low-resource lobbies?

Yes. Set Start Age to "Feudal or later" and Resources to "Medium or more". Treaty length, starting age and resources are also shown on every lobby card.

How often is the list updated?

Lobbies and player ratings refresh automatically every few seconds. You don't need to reload the page.

Where does the data come from?

Lobby data comes from the game's public lobby service and player ratings from aoe2lobby.com. AoE2Lobby.online is a fan-made site and isn't affiliated with Microsoft, Xbox Game Studios, World's Edge or Forgotten Empires.
