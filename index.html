Frequently asked questions

How do I join a lobby?

Press Join Game on a lobby card. Your browser opens Age of Empires II: DE and joins that lobby; the game needs to be installed and you need to be signed in. Lobbies with no open slots show Lobby Full, and lobbies marked Password ask for the password when you join.

Can I watch games that already started?

Yes. Open Spectate to see games in progress, ranked and custom, with each team's players, ratings and civilizations. Press Spectate on a game to watch it in Age of Empires II: DE. Games have a short spectator delay, usually 2 minutes, and the button shows a countdown until it ends. A lobby leaves the lobby list as soon as its game starts and appears under Spectate instead. Press Details (or the map name) for the game's own page, with a link you can share; once the game ends it shows the winner, the rating changes and a replay download. Hover over or tap a civilization to see its bonuses, unique units and unique techs.

Can I watch a whole game on the site?

Yes, once it has ended: the replay viewer plays back a game from its replay file, in your browser. It shows the map, every building as it is placed, where players sent their units, each player's resources in the bank and their units and buildings (the game's own figures, from every few seconds to every two minutes), ages, units and techs ordered, actions per minute, market trades, tributes and the chat. Press Watch this game on a finished game's page, search for a player on the replay viewer to pick any of their recent games, or drop in one of your own replays (the game saves them in Games\Age of Empires 2 DE\<your id>\savegame in your user folder) or the ZIP from a finished game's Download replay. A file you drop in is read on your computer and never uploaded. Units on the map are estimates, drawn walking to where they were sent. Real positions and fights only exist inside the game itself.

You can also follow a game while it's running, if you're watching it in Age of Empires II on the same computer. Open the replay viewer in Chrome or Edge and pick your savegame folder: the page follows the recording as the game writes it, with the whole game from its start to scrub through. It only works for games you're watching. A recording holds every player's orders, so the page refuses a game you're playing until it has ended. A game being shared on the site shows Watch live here on its page, and anyone can follow it with no game at all, at least 2 minutes behind the real game so players can't scout their own match.

Can I get an alert when a player starts a game?

Yes. Press the star (☆) next to a player's name on Spectate or on a game's page to follow them. While AoE2Lobby.online is open in a tab, you get an alert when they start a game, with links to the game's page and to watch it in game. The live game data is refreshed about once a minute, so alerts come a minute or two after the game starts. Spectate lists the players you follow and which of them are in a game; turn on desktop notifications there to be alerted while the tab is in the background (on a computer; most phone browsers can't show them from a web page).

Is there a scoreboard overlay for casters?

Yes. On a game's page, open Caster tools to set a title, the series length (best of), the score and the side names, then copy the overlay link into an OBS Browser source at 1920 × 1080. It shows the players, civilizations, ratings and score over your stream on a transparent background. It can follow one player from game to game, so the players and civilizations update by themselves. The score only changes when you change it: after each game, change it in Caster tools, copy the link again and paste it into the source's URL.

Which ELO is shown for each player?

By default the Unranked rating is shown, the same default aoe2lobby.com uses. Use Elo Type to switch to Random Map 1v1, Random Map Team, Empire Wars 1v1 or Empire Wars Team, or to Auto, which picks the ladder that matches the lobby. A game's own page (Details) always shows the ratings of the game's own ladder, named at the top of the page. Quick Play has a rating of its own that the live data doesn't include, so Quick Play games show no ratings there.

Can I hide Dark Age or low-resource lobbies?

Yes. Set Start Age to "Feudal or later" and Resources to "Medium or more". Treaty length, starting age and resources are also shown on every lobby card.

How often is the list updated?

Lobbies and player ratings refresh automatically every few seconds. You don't need to reload the page.

Where does the data come from?

The lobby list comes from the game's public lobby service. Lobby settings (such as starting age, resources, treaty and mods), AI slots, player ratings, Steam links and the Spectate list come from aoe2lobby.com, and map names from aoe2companion.com. Results of finished games come from the game's public match history, and replay downloads are the official aoe.ms links. Civilization bonuses and unit stats, and the replay viewer's unit, building and tech names and costs, come from the open-source aoe2techtree project (MIT licence). The replay viewer reads replay files the way the open-source mgz parser does (MIT licence). AoE2Lobby.online is a fan-made site and isn't affiliated with Microsoft, Xbox Game Studios, World's Edge or Forgotten Empires.

Privacy and contact

AoE2Lobby.online has no accounts and sets no cookies. What it stores in your browser stays there: your filter and view choices (aoe2State), the players you follow (aoe2Follows) and your caster tools settings (aoe2Caster) in local storage, so they are still set on your next visit, and the games a tab has already alerted you about (aoe2Alerted, in session storage, gone when the tab closes). Clearing this site's data removes them all. Your follows are never sent to the server: the page fetches the list of games in progress and looks for the players you follow itself. Desktop notifications appear only after you allow them in your browser. A replay file you drop into the replay viewer, or a savegame folder you let it read to follow a game, is read in your browser and never sent anywhere. Watch this game asks our server to fetch that game's official replay, which it keeps in memory for up to 30 minutes, so people opening the same game share one download. A player search sends the name you type to the game's public match history, and the answer is kept in memory for a minute. A game shared live is held in memory while it runs and for 10 minutes after. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless.

Player names, ratings and Steam links come from the sources above: the game's public lobby service and aoe2lobby.com. Results and rating changes of finished games come from the game's public match history (aoe2companion.com is only used to look up map names). The server keeps player profiles in memory for up to a day after a player was last seen in a lobby, plus a file with the current lobby list that is overwritten every few seconds. Games in progress, and games that ended in the last 2 hours, are also kept in memory, with their players' names, countries, Steam links, ratings and results, and each one's match page shows them for that time. Its access logs keep visitors' IP addresses masked (the last part removed) for 30 days.
